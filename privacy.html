Privacy Policy

OpenShorts Self-Publisher · Last updated: 17 August 2026

OpenShorts Self-Publisher ("the app") is a self-hosted tool operated by an individual creator on their own computer. It converts that creator's own long-form video recordings into short vertical clips and publishes them to that creator's own TikTok account.

The app has no public sign-up and no other end users. The only person who authenticates is the operator, using their own TikTok account. The app never accesses data belonging to any other TikTok user.

1. Who is responsible

Operator: Mykyta Vinnyk
Contact: developer@svitix.com

2. What data the app handles

  • TikTok OAuth tokens. An access token and refresh token issued for the operator's own account, plus the associated open_id. These are used solely to authorise uploads.
  • Basic account information returned by creator_info: nickname, avatar URL, permitted privacy options, and maximum allowed video duration. This is read so the app does not attempt an upload the account is not allowed to make.
  • Video files and captions created by the operator from their own recordings, together with the scheduled publication time.

3. Where data is stored

All of the above is stored locally on the operator's own machine, inside a container volume on that machine. Nothing is stored on any server operated by the app, because the app operates no servers. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no profiling.

4. Who data is shared with

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Data leaves the operator's machine only in these cases:

  • TikTok. Video files, captions and publishing options are sent to the TikTok Content Posting API in order to publish them, which is the entire purpose of the app.
  • Google Gemini API. The separate clip-selection stage of the pipeline sends the text transcript of the operator's own recording to Google's Gemini API to identify which moments to cut. No TikTok data and no TikTok tokens are ever sent to Google.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens remain on the operator's machine until they expire or are deleted. The operator can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok's own settings, and can delete all stored data by removing the container volume. Revoking access in TikTok immediately invalidates the stored tokens.

6. Security

Credentials are held in a local environment file and a local volume that are not published and not committed to source control. The app communicates with TikTok over HTTPS only.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by its operator.
