OpenShorts Self-Publisher · Last updated: 17 August 2026

1. What this is

OpenShorts Self-Publisher ("the app") is a self-hosted tool run by an individual creator on their own hardware. It cuts that creator's own long-form recordings into short vertical clips and uploads them to that creator's own TikTok account, either as drafts for manual confirmation or, where authorised, as scheduled posts.

The app is not a commercial service. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, charges nothing, and has no users other than its operator.
